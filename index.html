Who runs childcare at California's public universities

UCLA will close its campus childcare program in June 2027. Across all 33 UC and Cal State campuses, who runs childcare depends on the system: student government at most Cal State campuses, the university itself at most UC campuses.

By Jackson Mills · Draft as of Nov. 18, 2026

      About this data

      • Compiled from campus websites, student-government records, state licensing data, U.S. Education Department grant lists and news coverage, as of Nov. 6, 2026.
      • "Student government or campus nonprofit" includes Associated Students organizations and campus auxiliaries such as Fresno State Programs for Children.
      • Campuses are grouped by who runs their main program. Some have more than one, such as SF State's student-run center and its faculty-focused Children's Campus.
      • Licensed capacity is incomplete: licenses have not yet been found for many campuses, and some listings disagree.
      • Nothing here has been confirmed with the campuses yet. Every record is a starting point for reporting.
