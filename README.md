# Who runs childcare at California's public universities (draft)

A draft data visualization for editor review. UCLA plans to close its campus childcare program on June 30, 2027. This page maps who runs childcare at all 33 University of California and California State University campuses.

**Status:** Preliminary and unverified. Not for publication.

## What's here

- `index.html`: the whole page. Data, styles and code are in this one file, with no outside dependencies.

## What the page shows

1. Who runs childcare on each campus (university, student government, private contractor), split by UC and CSU
2. Licensed childcare slots found so far, with infant slots marked
3. UCLA's key budget numbers
4. A searchable record for every campus, with sources and open reporting questions

## Data notes

- Compiled from campus websites, student-government records, state licensing data, U.S. Education Department grant lists and news coverage, as of Nov. 6, 2026.
- Most licensed capacities come from third-party sites that republish California Department of Social Services data. Each still needs confirmation in the state's Care Facility Search: https://www.ccld.dss.ca.gov/carefacilitysearch/
- Nothing has been confirmed with the campuses yet.
